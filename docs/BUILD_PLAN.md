## Cross-cutting stop conditions

- Do not call a milestone locally verified unless its gate actually ran and evidence is linked in the status/register.
- Do not label synthetic AI/audio/delivery as live, or a server receipt as an assessment.
- Do not publish a version when any selected recipient language lacks required artifacts; do not silently omit recipients.
- Do not let worker DTOs or URLs reveal private rubric, correct-answer flags, staff notes, coworker information or internal jobs.
- Do not let stale jobs mutate a newer draft or any published version.
- Do not state that a matching answer proves comprehension or safety authorization.
- Do not treat screenshots, visual baselines, compile success or tests as real-device, live-provider or pilot authorization evidence.
